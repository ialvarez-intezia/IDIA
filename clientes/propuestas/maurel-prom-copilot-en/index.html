Without structured training, the team across both sites uses it sporadically or not at all, and the company leaves on the table the productivity it already has available.

Diagnosis

  1. The team uses Microsoft 365 every day, but Copilot remains unactivated or underused.
  2. Without a method for writing instructions (prompting), results depend on who tries it and how.
  3. Repetitive tasks in Word, Excel, and Outlook are still done by hand.
  4. Meeting minutes and follow-up in Teams don't take advantage of what Copilot can already solve.
  5. Leadership wants a deliberate rollout across Caracas and Maracaibo, not an activation without support.

02 · Objectives

Where we're taking
the team.

General objective

Develop in Maurel & Prom Venezuela's team the ability to use Microsoft Copilot within its Microsoft 365 environment, through deliberate practice with real company cases, to gain productivity in writing, analysis, and meeting minutes with responsible use of information.

Specific objectives

  1. Understand Copilot fundamentals and apply effective prompting in Copilot Chat.
  2. Integrate Copilot into real tasks in Word, Excel, Outlook, and Teams.
  3. Build a first agent of your own and get introduced to the flows that automate repetitive tasks.
  4. Adopt a common protocol for verification, privacy, and best practices, replicable across the whole team.
