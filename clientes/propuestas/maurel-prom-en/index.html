Maurel & Prom needs a plan that governs AI usage, not scattered rules.

Maurel & Prom Venezuela has an active Copilot license within its Microsoft 365 environment, but with no real adoption yet. Its Legal and Compliance team needs AI usage to be governed by clear rules, aligned with its parent company's regulatory framework in France, under a single plan rather than addressed separately.

Diagnosis

  1. Maurel & Prom has close to 50 people across 8 areas, between Caracas and Maracaibo, with no formal assessment of AI usage.
  2. The team has no clear criteria for what information can or cannot be shared with AI tools.
  3. Copilot is already licensed within the Microsoft 365 environment, but with no real adoption or associated training.
  4. The parent company in France requires the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Artificial Intelligence Act (AI Act), which Venezuela does not yet have.
  5. A plan is missing that connects assessment, training, policy, and innovation, with explicit value at every phase.

General objective

Take Maurel & Prom Venezuela from scattered AI usage to a complete, sustained organizational strategy: assess all 8 areas, train them with a complete package derived from that assessment, formalize correct and conscious AI usage backed by the European regulatory framework, and estimate the ongoing support that sustains maturity over time.
